Chocolate Muffins

Expected Time: 30 Minutes

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Baking Soda
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Milk & Eggs
  • Chocolate Chips
  • Cocoa Powder

    • Recipie

    • Whisk dry ingredients: flour, sugar, baking soda, cocoa powder, and salt
    • Mix wet ingredients: oil, egg, milk, and vanilla
    • Combine gently
    • Fold in chocolate chips and continue to mix
    • Fill shape molds and add sugar
    • Bake until golden brown (350F)
