Blueberry Muffins

Expected Time: 30 Minutes

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Baking Powder
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Milk & Eggs
  • Blueberries

    • Recipie

    • Whisk dry ingredients: flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt
    • Mix wet ingredients: oil, egg, milk, and vanilla
    • Combine gently
    • Fold in berries and continue to mix
    • Fill shape molds and add sugar
    • Bake until golden brown (400F)
