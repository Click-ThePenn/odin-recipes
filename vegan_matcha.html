Vegan Matcha Labubu Muffins

Expected Time: 1 Second

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Organic Sugar
  • Baking Powder
  • Matcha Powder
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Soy Milk & Flax Eggs
  • Almonds
  • Labubus

    • Recipie

    • Whisk dry ingredients: flour, sugar, baking powder, matcha powder, and salt
    • Sift the matcha powder
    • Mix wet ingredients: oil, flax eggs, soy milk, and vanilla
    • Combine gently
    • Fold in almonds and continue to mix
    • Fill shape molds and add sugar
    • Bake until golden brown (375F)
    • Top with labubus
